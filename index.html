Browser miner. Not the real thing.

Experiment: this page tries RandomX in a Web Worker and sends shares to MoneroOcean under your wallet via a small origin-locked VPS bridge (not Cloudflare). It is a sandbox next to the Android app. Expect toy H/s. iOS will suspend it in the background.

Before you start

  • You start it. Nothing hashes until you press Start. Closing the tab stops it.
  • Your wallet, your coins. The address goes to the pool as the stratum login. VoltMiner never holds XMR.
  • Ad-blockers will fight this. uBlock Origin and some antivirus flag Wasm miners even with consent. Pause them on this page or the WebSocket never opens.
  • Leave cores free. Default is half your threads, minus one. A frozen tab is not a faster miner.
  • RandomX in a tab, not XMRig. Same algo family as the APK (rx/0), different binary: Wasm light/cache mode, no huge pages. The Android app will always win on the same machine.
  • Needs the VPS bridge. Production is wss://bridge.voltminer.net on DigitalOcean. Not a Cloudflare Worker.
  • Needs compiled Wasm. Until js/vendor/randomx/web-randomx.wasm is built, the worker will connect and receive jobs but will not submit shares.
