Browser miner. Made for Desktops/Laptops/Chromebooks

A standalone experiment: this page runs RandomX in a Web Worker and sends shares to MoneroOcean under your wallet. We take 0% — no cut of your hashrate, no redirected shares (the pool may charge its own fee). Expect toy H/s. A hidden tab stops hashing.

Before you start

  • You start it. Nothing hashes until you press Start. Closing the tab stops it.
  • Your wallet, your coins. The address goes to the pool as the stratum login. VoltMiner never holds XMR.
  • 0% fee. We take no cut of the hashrate and never swap your payout address. MoneroOcean may apply its own pool fee.
  • Ad-blockers will fight this. uBlock Origin and some antivirus flag Wasm miners even with consent. Pause them on this page or the WebSocket never opens.
  • Desktop browser. Fast mode pins about 3.5 GB inside the tab, so 8 GB machines may swap or fall back to cache-only mode. iOS and Safari usually suspend the worker.
  • One dataset, extra cores. This page is cross-origin isolated, so the C++ pthreads share a single ~2 GB dataset — each extra thread adds only its scratchpad. Interpreted Wasm: tens of H/s, not thousands.
  • Keep the tab visible. Background tabs pause Workers. Do not mash Start — extra logins can get the relay rate-limited.
  • RandomX, interpreted. Same algorithm family as native miners (rx/0), but no huge pages and no JIT in Wasm.
  • Why a relay. A browser cannot open the pool's TLS stratum socket, so a small origin-locked relay on our VPS forwards the WebSocket to the pool. It does not rewrite your login, the job, or the shares in either direction.

Standard (95) or integrated (106) Monero address. Never a private key.

What this page does — and what it does not

        
This is a consented cryptominer; that is the whole point of the page. Here is the short version,
          and the commands to check it yourself.

        

          

            It does
            
    
              
  • Start hashing only after you press Start, and stop on Stop or when the tab closes.
    • 
              
  • Send shares to MoneroOcean with your address as the pool login — the one you typed.
    • 
              
  • Write one thing to this site's private storage: a ~2 GB RandomX dataset cache, reused so it is not rebuilt every visit.
    • 
              
  • Remember two small things locally: your address, and the VMW-… worker id.
    • 
              
  • Open a single WebSocket to this site's own relay, which forwards bytes to the pool unchanged.
    • 
            

          

          

            It does not
            
    
              
  • Ask for a seed phrase or private key. Ever. Anyone who does is stealing from you.
    • 
              
  • Mine before you press Start, mine with the tab closed, install anything, or run as a background service.
    • 
              
  • Load third-party scripts, fonts, analytics or ads — the CSP header forbids it (default-src 'none').
    • 
              
  • Use cookies, or ask for camera, microphone, location or your files.
    • 
              
  • Touch anyone's coins: mining pays the address you pasted, and no key is ever involved.
    • 
            

          

        

        
Check the bytes you were served yourself:
          curl -s https://web.voltminer.net/js/vendor/randomx/web-randomx-mt.wasm | shasum -a 256
          — published hashes, the wasm patch, the build recipe and the relay's source live in
          /transparency. The relay rewrites nothing, so a redirected payout would show up as
          somebody else's address on the MoneroOcean dashboard.
