RandomX — two builds

Two binaries ship, both the same algorithm and both producing identical hashes. The shared-memory js/vendor/randomx/web-randomx-mt.{js,wasm} (pthread build) is built from github.com/aa022/RandomX-bonanza at commit f9848e3 — the RandomX engine plus a WebAssembly JIT backend — with Emscripten 4.0.10 and the patches in third_party/RandomX-JIT-overlay/. The single-thread js/vendor/randomx/web-randomx.{js,wasm} is built from github.com/WebCryptomining/WebRandomX at commit 780908a with the patches in third_party/WebRandomX-overlay/. Relay (server side, not shipped to browsers)

The origin-locked WebSocket→TLS relay uses the ws package for Node.js (MIT, © 2011 Einar Otto Stangvik and contributors). It never runs in your browser and never touches your wallet.

No fee

VoltMiner takes 0% of your hashrate on this page. Your wallet is the pool login; we do not redirect shares, and the relay rewrites nothing in either direction. MoneroOcean may charge its own pool fee, which is outside our control.
